# Song practice maps — 75 references

Each of the 15 grooves has five authored practice loops. Complexity 0–3 simplifies the chosen loop, 4 restores its reference section and articulation/dynamics, and 5–7 adds labeled genre exercises. Other sliders, personal-kit substitutions and manual notes can change that target.

These are short practice arrangements rather than complete note-for-note transcriptions. In particular, percussion ensembles and compound/odd-rock examples may require explicit kit or meter adaptations listed below. The reference recording/lesson is separate from the BPM source: a tempo catalog does not prove the drum transcription. BPM is approximate; live performances can drift. The Spotify catalog can use a remaster/compilation of the recording; compare with YouTube and use Tap tempo if versions differ.

Nirvana follows the middle chorus bar in [Drumeo Level 5](https://www.drumeo.com/beat/?p=24183). Phil Collins is a looped adaptation of the iconic tom entrance, not the sparse verse; Antmusic reduces multiple drummers to one kit. Footprints and 7/4 adaptations preserve explicitly documented pulse/bar conversions.
